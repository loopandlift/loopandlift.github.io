Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Loop & Lift is free while it's in Early Access. The full game is planned at $19.99 when it launches.

Why do I need an account?

So your parks' backups, your Founding Tester number and your feedback stay yours, and every update reaches you. It takes a minute: a username, a password and the code we email you.

Why does Windows warn me?

Loop & Lift is a brand-new game by a small team, and Windows warns about any program it hasn't seen many people download yet. The installer only puts the game in your user folder.

Can I share videos and screenshots?

Please do! To share the game itself, send friends this page, so they always get the newest version.

Where are my parks saved?

On your computer, in %APPDATA%\com.loopandlift.game\saves. They stay when the game updates.

What do you collect?

Your account, how you play (time, rides you build, frame rate, crashes) and copies of your parks, to fix bugs and make the game better. Nothing outside the game. Details in the Privacy Notice and the Terms.

Mac or phone?

Not yet: Windows first. Follow @playloopandlift to hear when.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Loop and Lift, Uninstall.
