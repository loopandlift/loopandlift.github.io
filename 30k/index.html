Shape every curve

Drag a curve, stretch a drop, roll it upside down. Loops, corkscrews, cobra rolls, launches and splashdowns flow into one smooth ride, and the G-force view shows what your riders will feel.

02

Take the front seat

Every coaster you build can be ridden from any seat. Hang over the top of a 139 m tower, or watch the train from the ground like a TV crew filming it.

A park that's alive

Thousands of guests walk your paths, queue, eat and scream. Kids run to the gentle rides, thrill seekers to your tallest coaster. Keep them happy and the money rolls in.

Castles, caves, waterfalls, fairy tale and desert island scenery, bridges and lakes. Shape the land, paint the ground and build a park nobody else has.
