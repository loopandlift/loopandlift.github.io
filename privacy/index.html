Loop & Lift Beta Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-10

This explains what the Loop & Lift beta collects, why, and what you can do about it. The beta is run by the developer of Loop & Lift, who decides how this data is used.

What is collected

  • Your account: username, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by the hosting provider), country, and anything optional you add (Instagram name, how you heard about the game, invite code), plus the boxes you ticked and when.
  • Your computer: a code made from your computer's id (scrambled, so the real id never leaves your computer), Windows version, graphics card name, number of processor cores, screen size, and the game's graphics settings and frame rate.
  • How you play: when you play and for how long, how long you spend building, designing coasters, riding or in photo mode, which rides, shops and coasters you build (with their stats), errors and crashes in the game.
  • Your parks: copies of the parks you save, with pictures of them.
  • Feedback you send: your message, a screenshot of the game, and, if you choose, your park, plus the game's recent log.
  • Technical logs: the hosting provider keeps your IP address in its server logs for a short time.

Nothing outside the game is collected: no files, other programs, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Why

To run the beta (your account, approving testers, checking your pass, keeping the game for testers only), to find and fix bugs and slow spots, to learn what players enjoy and improve the game, and to answer your feedback. The legal basis is running the beta you signed up for, and my legitimate interest in improving the game. With your permission (a separate box) your parks may appear in Loop & Lift posts and videos with your username, and you may get news by email.

Who else handles it

The data is stored with Supabase (database and file hosting) and the game is downloaded from GitHub. They process it for me under their own security and privacy terms and may store it outside your country. It is never sold, and there are no ads or ad trackers in the game.

How long

Until you delete your account, or at the latest 90 days after the beta ends (unless you keep your account for the full game).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or get a copy of your data, object to its use, or have it deleted. To have your account, parks, pictures and feedback deleted, reply to any Loop & Lift email or message @loopandlift on Instagram, and it's done. For anything else, use Send feedback in the game too. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority).

Children

The beta is for people 16 and older, or younger with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the game will show you the new version.
