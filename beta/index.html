The game opens on the station. Pick a username and a password, type in the code we email you, and you're on the list.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The beta is free for testers.

Why does Windows warn me?

Loop & Lift is a brand-new game by one developer, and Windows warns about any program it hasn't seen many people download yet. The installer only puts the game in your user folder.

Can I share videos and screenshots?

Please do! Just don't share the installer or your account: every tester needs their own pass.

Where are my parks saved?

On your computer, in %APPDATA%\com.loopandlift.game\saves. They stay when the game updates.

What do you collect?

Your account, how you play (time, rides you build, frame rate, crashes) and copies of your parks, to fix bugs and make the game better. Nothing outside the game. The details are in the Privacy Notice and the Beta Terms. Ask any time and your account is deleted.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Loop and Lift, Uninstall.

Mac or phone?

Not yet: Windows first. Follow @loopandlift to hear when.
